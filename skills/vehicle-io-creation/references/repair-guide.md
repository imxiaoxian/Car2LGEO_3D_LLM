# Repair guide

- Floating wheel or tyre: inspect native hub/tyre origins, axle direction and port positions. Correct the transform or use a compatible pair; never scale the visible mesh independently of connectors.
- For a disconnected shaft, read `check.shaft_diagnostics` for the actual world endpoints, lateral error, axial gap and engagement. These bounded suggestions do not certify an insertion or clearance. Moving a subtree that contains both endpoints cannot repair their relative gap. Move the appropriate independent module or change the local transform, preserving the intended rolling/steering joint ownership.
- Split door/window: place glass in the door subtree and check fixed quarter-window ownership. Verify the common joint throughout its range.
- Cabin/front/rear gaps: inspect shared module interfaces and real geometry. Adjust layout or select compatible panels, preserving required detail.
- Collision: locate instances and collider evidence, then check native origins and keyed orientation. Broad shapes can overestimate curved contacts; record ambiguity as unverified until refined geometry establishes clearance.
- Missing data: resolve the dependency with its hash or use a verified alternative. Missing evidence cannot pass by omission.
- Export mismatch: compare recursive world transforms, colors, steps and BOM; retain unknown archive members. Never overwrite the source corpus.
- Repeated failure: after three module repairs, preserve the draft, diagnostics and successful modules. Do not invent accepted appearance examples or passing reports.
