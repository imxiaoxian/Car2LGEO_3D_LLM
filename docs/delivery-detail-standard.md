# Delivery Detail Standard

The user confirmed on 2026-10-19 that each knowledge-base IO defines the
required appearance and structural detail level. Full real-car reconstruction
is not the delivery standard. This supersedes earlier universal demands for
hidden mechanisms, microscopic interior fittings and separate optical layers.

## Required Quality

Match recognizable proportions, silhouette, body continuity, visible cabin,
lamps, windows/openings, wheels and distinctive features at baseline detail.
Check all included parts for native dimensions, mounting, clearance and missing
dependencies. Any moving glass belongs to its door. Preserve the mechanisms
represented by the baseline; do not invent requirements from real-car photos.
Digital, appearance, Studio and physical validation remain separate.

## Scope Changes

- No universal four-spring suspension requirement. Source part descriptions
  identify four shock absorbers in R34; none were identified in the other three.
- F40's steering wheel does not establish working steering. The contract no
  longer universally demands a steering rack for that model.
- Countach and F40 door sections contain no independently transparent parts.
  A separate glass pane is not a universal door role. Any included glazing
  still receives ownership, visibility, contact and clearance checks.
- Match the baseline lamp representation. Separate reflectors, transparent
  covers and actuators are not universally mandatory components.
- Hidden pedal hardware, seat rails and the real car's exact rim-hole count
  do not become required merely because they appear in a reference photograph.
- Three exact Countach five-opening rim findings are recorded as out of scope.
  Original review verdicts and source images remain unchanged. Other wheel
  defects cannot inherit that exclusion.

## Reference Scope

New plans for the four targets omit the hidden pedal-footwell and seat-mount
shots. The Countach plan contains 30 shots instead of 32; existing images and
their original reviews stay available. Visible seats, dashboard, body, wheels,
lamps and door ownership remain in scope. Lamp prompts request the baseline's
visible representation, rather than mandatory independent optical internals.
Running-gear prompts include steering and suspension only when required by
that target's baseline. R34 and Chiron cover images remain synthetic design
options pending source articulation review, not proof of a required mechanism.

Chinese/English brand names, combined brand/model identities and unambiguous
model-only identities resolve to the same detail profile. An explicitly
different brand cannot inherit another target's profile. Generic vehicles
outside these four retain their existing requirements.
